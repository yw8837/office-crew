# 0017: Goal mode researches and holds a meeting before the work, every turn in a new conversation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20 (research runs read-only, research note counts and goal meeting prompts added 2026-11-22)
- Related: [0005](0005-meeting-stage.md), [0010](0010-meetings-take-turns.md) and [0011](0011-meetings-pause-for-research.md) (the meeting engine), [0007](0007-local-only-user-messages.md) (nobody from outside the team), [0014](0014-goal-mode-confirmed-goal-card.md) (the goal card and confirming), [0015](0015-goal-mode-fresh-review.md) (run once, the call cap), [0016](0016-goal-mode-continue-a-cut-goal.md) (the cut point), `server/mission.js` (`prepPlan`, `prepCard`, `prepFits`, `firstJudge`, `meetingCalls`, `meetingCallsMin`, `instructionLine`, `answerFlags`, `dropInstructionLines`, `guardOf`, `researchGuards`, `parseResearch`, `cleanQuestions`, `researchBlock`, `scanPrompt`, `questionsPrompt`, `researchPrompt`, `researchNote`, `conclusionPrompt`, `resumePoint`, `stageDiff`, `pastOutputs`), `server/orchestrator.js` (`#goalTurn`, `#goalPrompt`, `confirmMission`, `resumeMission`, `#missionPlan`, `#missionRun`, `#prepRun`, `#scanStage`, `#questionsStage`, `#researchStage`, `#researchCard`, `#prepMeeting`, `#prepRefs`, `#runStage`, `#meeting`, `#meetingPrompt`, `#runAgent`, `#system`, `#prompt`, `#reviewStage`, `#reviewedBy`, `#withMissed`, `handoffWriter`, `#autoWriter`, `#remember`, `control`), `server/store.js` (`#metaOf`), `server/index.js` (the 409 body), `server/providers/mock.js`, `public/js/views/mission.js` (`GoalCard`, `ResearchCard`, `MissionLog`, `resumeFrom`), `public/js/views/session.js`, `public/js/chat-tools.js` (`hideResearch`), `test/mission-prep.test.js`, `test/fake-cli/fake-claude.js`

## Context

After 0014 to 0016 a confirmed goal goes straight into the team's stage list. Nobody first looks up what the goal depends on (rules, similar cases, competitors, what users complain about), and nothing decides what to make before making it; for a contest entry that is most of the work. Meetings so far continue each participant's session conversation: in a long session one meeting turn cost about 12 dollars (API price, 2026-11-11 run), and the turns stay in the agents' conversations.

## Options

1. Leave research and meetings to the team's own stages. A team may have none, and their answers would reach later stages as earlier results, not marked as reference.
2. Let the lead decide during the run what to research and whether to meet. The order and the number of calls can't be predicted or tested, and 0014 keeps the order in code.
3. Fixed steps in code before the stage list (scan, questions, research per question, a research note, a meeting), each one the user can switch off on the card; every turn runs once in a new conversation; their text reaches the work only as marked reference.

## Decision

Option 3.

- **Steps and order** (fixed in code): with research on, **Scan** (the first research agent, one call: the topic, the current state, sources to start from, assumptions to check) → **Research questions** (the lead writes 2 to 6 questions in one fenced `oc-research` JSON block at the end of the answer; questions are made one line, up to 200 characters, duplicates dropped, at most 6; one question is accepted; a block that can't be read is asked for once more in the same run with a new message, and if that fails too, or the lead ends in an error or is skipped, the run goes on with one question, "Facts we need to know first to reach this goal", and says so) → **Research** (one call per question; questions are handed to the research agents in turn, agents work at the same time, one agent's questions one after another) → a research note. With the meeting on, **Pre-work meeting** (the lead as chair, the 0010 and 0011 engine as it is, 2 rounds; named so it isn't confused with a team stage called "Meeting"). Then the team's stages and the criteria check (0015). These are stages at the front of the work run with `mission: 'scan'|'questions'|'research'|'meeting'`.
- **Who**: research agents are every team member with a research role, in team order; meeting participants are the lead and the team members who aren't builders or reviewers, in team order, at most 5. Nobody outside the team (so a team of local models stays local, 0007). The lead writes the questions and chairs the meeting, so neither step can run when the lead is no longer in the team (for example taken out after the card was drafted).
- **The card**: `prep: { direct, lead: { id, name, inTeam }, research: { can, on, who, min, max }, meeting: { can, on, who, rounds, calls, checks } }`. Both boxes are on by default, and off by default when the lead put `"direct": true` in the goal block (the goal prompt mentions it only to teams that can do either step); the card then says the lead suggested starting right away. A step the team can't do is locked with the reason (no research role; fewer than two participants; the lead no longer in the team). The card shows an estimate for the chosen steps (research 3 to 8 calls: the scan, the questions and one call per question, 1 to 6 questions; asking again for a block or an empty answer, or retrying, costs more). Confirming sends `prep: { research, meeting, lead, researchers, attendees }` (the boxes, the lead and the lists the card showed); a step the team can't do now, or a lead or list that differs from the current one, gets 409 with the current `prep`, and nothing starts. A confirm without `prep` (an older screen) runs no research and no meeting. The goal stores `mission.prep`; the confirmed card keeps `prepChosen`.
- **Run once, new conversations**: every call in these steps uses the run-once mode of 0015 (no conversation id read or saved, no usage baseline read or written, no role-note hash, no earlier conversation, memory, handoff note or whole-conversation file, no question card, no queued instructions), and their answers, the pre-work meeting's turns included, are marked `prep` and kept out of project memory (a team's own meeting stage in a goal still goes into memory as before). Every meeting inside a goal's work run, the pre-work meeting and the team's own meeting stages (with their research turns), runs once too: each turn gets the agenda, the research note when there is one, and everything said so far, never the participant's earlier conversation. Meetings outside goal mode are unchanged. Messages that ran once are marked `once`; like review answers they don't count as an agent's own last answer (missed conversation, handoff writer). The steps' answers and a goal's meeting turns (the conclusion too; a team meeting stage's conclusion still goes to the next stage as that stage's result) are also left out of every conversation handed to agents (`[Previous conversation]`, the conversation an agent missed, the picked parts and the whole-conversation file, the API history), for agents with and without a CLI conversation and after continuing a cut goal: text read outside could be in them, and they reach the work only through the marked note, conclusion and previous stage results. A team meeting's turns in a goal still go into project memory; an agent continuing its own conversation later still gets its own one-off turns from memory (marked), since its conversation doesn't hold them.
- **Research and meeting turns run read-only** (decided by the user, 2026-11-22): the scan, the research questions turn, each research question, and every turn of the pre-work meeting and of a team's own meeting stage in a goal's work run (opinions, turns, verdicts, the conclusion and research during the meeting) run with the agent's permission set to read-only (`plan`) for that call only, also after a takeover to another AI. The saved agent keeps its setting, and the work stages and the criteria check run with the agent's own permission (the app neither raises nor lowers a reviewer's permission). Meetings outside goal mode are unchanged. What that blocks depends on the AI: Claude gets `--permission-mode plan` and reads only the user's settings (`--setting-sources user`); Codex gets `sandbox_mode="read-only"` with hooks off; Gemini CLI gets `--approval-mode plan`; for an ACP agent the app grants only read, search, think and fetch requests and refuses file writes (no terminal). Hermes has no permission setting the app can pass, so its research is bound only by the written instruction; API models and models on this PC have no tools at all. The system instruction says so for each case: read-only, and for research "use any search or reading tools you have; if there are none, work from what you know and the files" (it doesn't promise web tools); "don't change files or run commands" for an AI with tools the app can't restrict; for research on an AI without tools, answer from what it knows. These turns don't get the line telling a read-only agent to read CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md first (a file read on every turn). The research note card names, separately, the agents whose research ran on an AI that can't be held read-only (bound only by the instruction: Hermes) and on an AI without tools (API models, models on this PC), counting every AI an answer went through after a takeover.
- **Prompts**: only the confirmed goal and criteria are instructions (the same block the work stages get), with the user's request and attached files. The scan, the questions the lead wrote, the research answers and the meeting conclusion are reference inside random markers. Research prompts ask for a source on every fact, "not checked" for what wasn't verified, and no file changes, one fact per line as "- fact | source (address or file) | checked" or "| not checked", and list the questions the other branches cover (inside the same markers) so the answers don't overlap. The questions prompt lists, for planning goals, rules and conditions / similar cases / competing services / past winners / user complaints, to use only where they fit.
- **Research note**: `mission.research = { scanMsgId, questions: [{ id: 'q1', text, agentId, status: 'pending'|'running'|'done'|'empty'|'failed'|'skipped', msgId, outside? }], noteMsgId, fallback? }`. A branch that fails, is skipped or answers empty is "not checked" and the others go on. The code then adds one card message `{ role: 'proposal', kind: 'research', status: 'done', text: '', missionId, total, missing, fallback?, questions: [{ id, text, agentId, status, msgId, excerpt, more, suspect, unsourced }], suspect, unsourced, textOnly?, noTools? }`: "n of m questions not checked", the first 1,200 characters of each answer and a link to the whole answer, marked "written by an agent · not confirmed by you". It is not passed to agents and not stored in memory. The code counts two numbers for the card and the note's head, with no extra call (`answerFlags`). Lines that look like instructions (`instructionLine`): after removing invisible and control characters, a line that matches the injection patterns of [0013](0013-agents-keep-role-notes.md) and reads as an instruction: a fake system tag or a "system prompt:" heading always; an English pattern only in a clause that starts with the ordering verb ("Sites share cookies" doesn't count); a Korean pattern not when it goes on as a condition or a description ("…하면", "…하는"); a line that only names a system prompt doesn't count. List lines without a source: no address (`https://…`, `www.…`), no `file:line` and no `path/file.ext`, and not saying "not checked" ("Node.js", "and/or", "24/7" and "I/O" are not sources). An answer with an instruction-like line gets a warning on the card; the work stages get the answer without those lines plus one line saying how many were left out, while the pre-work meeting gets it as it is, with a warning in its heading. In a goal's meetings a research result gets the same counts and AI marks on its message, and the following turns see it with the same warning.
- **Where it goes**: the research note (each answer's first 1,200 characters) goes to the pre-work meeting and every work stage; the conclusion (`mission.meetingMsgId`, first 2,000 characters) goes to every work stage; both as reference blocks, neither into the criteria check, and the steps are not repeated under "[Previous stage results]".
- **Meeting prompts in a goal** (the pre-work meeting and a team's own meeting stage in a goal's work run; meetings outside goal mode are unchanged): the opinion ends with a "Position:" line; every turn (where participants see each other's words, not the opinions of round 1) is told to write first what it doesn't agree with and, when it changes its position, what made it change; a turn, verdict or conclusion shows the participant's own earlier words under "[What you said earlier]", apart from the others' words (inside the same markers, and the warning before them says so); the opposing side is told to pick the strongest claim made so far and rebut it with evidence, not to oppose by tone. The chair doesn't speak first: round 1 is written by everyone at the same time without seeing each other (0010), and the chair takes no turn after that.
- **Skipping the turn rounds** (goal meetings only): if everyone, the opposing side included, takes the same position in round 1, the turn rounds are skipped. After round 1 the chair gives a verdict block (0010) on that question alone: agree only when the position of every participant, the opposing side included (it writes an independent opinion in round 1 too), points to the same option. On agree the chair concludes; otherwise the turns run as before, and the verdicts between turn rounds still leave the opposing side out (0010). The progress tab gets one line either way. This judgement is made only when the turn rounds it could skip cost more than one call (`firstJudge`): not for two participants and two rounds, where the turn round is a single call.
- **Extra instructions** sent during these steps are stored as before and go into the work stages, not into the research or the pre-work meeting. An instruction sent from the panel to an agent in one of these steps, or in a goal meeting turn (also before its CLI has started), becomes an extra instruction (there is no conversation to put it into).
- **Calls**: every call counts toward the goal's cap. Before each step the run checks what it needs: the scan 1, the questions 1, the research the open questions, the meeting `p + j + (rounds − 1)(p − 1) + max(0, rounds − 2) + 1` for `p` participants, where `j` is 1 when the chair judges after round 1 (the meeting engine's count without research turns; when everyone, the opposing side included, takes the same position in round 1 it is `p + 2`, `meetingCallsMin`, and the card says so), with the same cap question as 0015. A team's own meeting stage in a goal is checked against the same meeting count (other stages against their number of agents). Pausing takes effect before each research question too (one pause wait, even with several research agents); "Skip" without choosing an agent skips only the questions running at that moment, and the others are still researched. When the calls left are under a quarter of the cap, research during a goal meeting is turned off (a question block gets "not checked", `check.status: 'low'`, no call) with one log line per meeting.
- **Cut and continue** (0016): `mission.at.phase` can also be `scan`, `questions`, `research` or `meeting`. Continuing starts at that step; earlier steps don't run again; research runs only the questions that didn't finish (`pending`, `running`); a meeting starts again at round 1. A cap question is asked again without a call; any other wait in a step (an error in the meeting) redoes the step. No conversation is dropped and no "[Continuing]" line is added: everything in these steps ran once. The same holds for a team's own meeting stage cut in a goal: it starts again at round 1, and the participants' and the research agent's saved conversations are kept (the line about checking the work folder doesn't fit a meeting turn). The stage list check compares the steps' kinds and positions (not their labels).
- **Screen**: the stage strip shows "Scan → Research questions → Research ×n → Pre-work meeting → team stages → Criteria check"; the questions answer hides its block and lists the questions; research answers carry their question id; turns that ran once show "new conversation"; the research note card; the progress tab shows the choice, the note summary (or that research stopped, when the goal isn't running) and a line per step.
- The demo provider answers the scan, the questions and the research by their exact heads and closing block, so the steps can be tried at no usage. The fake Claude for tests reads `RQBAD`, `RQBAD2`, `RFAIL`, `REMPTY`, `DIRECT` and `SLOWSCAN`/`SLOWQ`/`SLOWR2`/`SLOWMEET`.

This adds `mission.prep`, `mission.research`, `mission.meetingMsgId` and four `at.phase` values; `prep`, `once`, `researchBlock`, `aiChain` (the AIs an answer went through after a takeover) and, for a research result in a goal's meeting, `flags`, `textOnly` and `noTools` on messages; `prep` and `prepChosen` on goal cards; `kind: 'research'` cards; `count` and four `mission` values on run stages. Older versions don't know them; they draw a research note card as "Hire proposal cancelled".

## Consequences

- Outside sources behind the 2026-11-22 additions: work on prompt injection treating marked blocks ("spotlighting") as a weak defence and recommending that an agent which read untrusted text be kept from consequential actions (arXiv 2403.14720, 2506.08837); and work on multi-agent debate showing that agents follow the majority when they can't see their own earlier answer, that tone instructions barely change choices, and that most of a debate's gain comes from the majority vote (arXiv 2610.02702, 2609.08016, 2508.17536). Only abstracts or summaries of the 2026 papers were read.

- A goal with both steps costs 2 + the questions (2 to 6) + the meeting (4 for two participants; 7 for three, or 5 when everyone, the opposing side included, takes the same position in round 1; plus up to 3 research turns) more calls; the demo team's goal went from 6 to 18 calls, and with the 2026-11-22 additions it is 16, since the demo chair agrees after round 1 (run with demo agents in the demo team's layout, three meeting participants).
- A goal's meetings no longer continue the participants' conversations: they don't pay for a long session's history in every turn, and they don't remember the meeting in their own conversation later (the work stages get the conclusion as reference).
- Not covered: the meeting can't ask the user and can't split into two options to compare. Task cards were added in [0018](0018-goal-mode-task-cards.md). Whether a read-only research agent can search the web depends on the CLI and isn't checked. Sources in research answers aren't checked by code. Read-only in these steps holds only as far as the AI enforces it (above); for Hermes "don't change files" is only an instruction. Extra instructions sent during the research or the pre-work meeting reach only the work stages. The cap check before a meeting doesn't count research turns, retries or takeovers, so those can go over it.
- Not covered: how real CLIs follow `oc-research` and how good their research is isn't measured; the tests use fake CLIs only.
- Not covered (2026-11-22): what each CLI's read-only mode lets through wasn't run with a real CLI. Whether Claude's plan mode in `-p` runs WebSearch and WebFetch without asking, whether Codex can search the web in a read-only sandbox (the app doesn't turn its web search on; that is the user's own Codex setting), and what Gemini CLI's plan mode allows are not checked. An ACP agent that runs a tool without asking for permission isn't stopped by the app. Hermes research is bound only by the instruction. The two counts are patterns: an instruction written as a description or a condition isn't counted, a harmless order-like line can be, and a source written in words ("the official site") or a bare file name counts as no source. The app doesn't count how many participants changed their position between rounds (it would need another AI call; only facts the code knows are counted).
- Screens checked in a real browser: with the demo agents (no AI usage): the research and meeting switches and the call estimate on the goal card, both switches off going straight to the team's stages, the stage strip from scan through questions, research and the pre-work meeting to the criteria check, the research note card with its two counts (0 and 0 once the demo answers carry addresses), the "pre-work meeting" name, the round-one check including the opposer skipping the turn rounds (in the chat and the progress tab), and the goal ending as passed. Not looked at: the card redrawn after a 409, the call cap question during research, the note card marks for AIs held by a written instruction only or without tools (the demo agents show neither), a goal cut during research or the meeting, the English, Chinese and Japanese screens, the dark theme and a narrow window
- To undo: confirm without `prep` (no step runs); for the meetings, drop the run-once choice for meeting turns in `#runAgent`.
