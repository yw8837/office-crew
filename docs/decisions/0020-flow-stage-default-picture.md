# 0020: The split view shows a flow stage above the chat; the village stays in the Large office view

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Related: [0009](0009-agents-ask-with-a-marked-block.md) (agent questions), [0010](0010-meetings-take-turns.md) and [0011](0011-meetings-pause-for-research.md) (meeting turns and research), [0014](0014-goal-mode-confirmed-goal-card.md) to [0019](0019-goal-mode-call-estimate-and-limit-wait.md) (goal mode: the card, the criteria check and returns, cut goals, task cards, the call cap and the usage-limit wait), `public/js/stage-model.js` (`stageModel`, `openQuestions`, `requestOf`, `meetLabel`, `goalWait`), `public/js/views/stage.js` (`FlowStage`, `StageStrip`), `public/css/stage.css`, `public/js/views/session.js` (`OfficePanel`, `VillagePanel`, `RunBar`, `SessionView`), `test/stage-model.test.js`

## Context

The picture above the chat was the village (or an office skin) in every view. It showed where each agent was, but not the order of the stages, how far the run had got, or where it waited for the user; that was in the stage strip of the bar below the chat, a long way from the picture. Much of what a run actually does had no place in the picture: a return after a failed criterion, the criteria verdicts, the call count, a goal card waiting to be confirmed, a goal cut off by closing the app, an automatic continue after a usage limit. And at the default height of the split view the characters were small.

## Options

1. Keep the village and tidy its labels, motion and colors. A small change, but the order of the stages stays out of the picture.
2. A flow stage: the stages as stations from left to right, the work card next to whoever holds it, a gate on the line where the run waits for the user, and the bar's stage strip moved into it. A larger change; the village's story is lost in the split view.
3. A thin strip only. The most room for the chat, but meetings and parallel work shrink to small marks.

## Decision

Option 2 for the split view, the folded shape of option 3 for the chat-only view, and the village kept as it is in the Large office view.

- **Views.** Split: the flow stage, 156 px by default; the handle resizes it from 120 to 240 px, and under 140 px it takes a small shape (names and the meeting line hidden, the top line shortened). Stations keep a minimum width; when they don't fit, only the row of stations scrolls sideways, to the current station (or, with none, to the your-turn gate), also after the window is resized, without smooth scrolling under reduced motion. The height is kept in the browser's storage as `ui.stageH`. The handle is a separator (with its height, minimum and maximum) that also moves with the up and down arrow keys, 8 px a press. Chat only: a 30 px strip with a segment per stage, the current stage, the "your turn" pill and a Show button. Large office: unchanged, the village or an office skin with the stage strip in the bar below. The flow stage is not a skin; the skin setting and its default (`village`) don't change, and `watch.js` doesn't change.
- **The bar below the chat.** In the split and chat-only views it keeps pause or resume, stop and end meeting, and the memory badge and session usage it already had; the stage strip and the finished chip move to the stage or the strip. In the Large office view it is unchanged.
- **One DOM-free model** (`stageModel`) built from the session's latest run, the live agent states, the goal, the open questions and the team, and nothing else:
  - Stations are the run's stages (so research, the pre-work meeting, the plan, task card steps and the criteria check appear as stations when the run has them). With no run, the team's stages as to-do; with no team, one station for the session's agent; while a goal card waits to be confirmed, the team's stages as to-do, since nothing has started.
  - A station's state comes from the stage status (to do, working, done, waiting, error, skipped, stopped); a stage waiting at a goal's verdict, block or usage limit shows as waiting (as the bar's stage strip did), and the stopped stage of a cut goal's own run as cut.
  - People's states are drawn only on the current station and on the station a wait refers to, and only those people (and a researcher away from a meeting) feed the activity line and the limit pill, so the state of an earlier run doesn't linger. The work card is held by everyone of the current station while it works or waits (the chair in a meeting; in a meeting stage's return, the person the server asks to redo it: the goal's lead when in that stage, else its first agent), also by someone who finished first in a parallel stage until the stage ends. An agent that was deleted shows as "Deleted agent" and can't be clicked.
  - A meeting comes from the stage's `meetingNow`: the round and phase, the chair, the opposer, who speaks (everyone in the opinion round), and an empty seat while a participant researches; the speaker's latest text is shown as text. The meeting keeps the speaker, the opposer, the chair and the researcher in front when there are more than four people. The criteria check station shows a chip per confirmed criterion (checking while it runs), on one line, the last chip turning into "+n" when they don't fit. The top line says "n criteria" until a verdict exists. A return shows "Return n/cap" on the stage that redoes the work while that stage is the current one (the server saves the goal's next place after the return without sending it, so a pause before the next check would otherwise keep the old mark). A stage note from the server (such as people skipped) is the station's tag.
  - At most four people per station, then "+n" (its label and the station's tooltip list every name); more than six stations fold the earlier ones, keeping one before the current station; stations still to come are never folded.
- **Your turn.** The pill (top right, and in the strip) and a yellow gate on the line, by kind and where the server asks: an approval after its stage; a goal verdict and unused extra instructions after the check station; a block after the work stage that reported it; a card retry limit and the call cap before the stage they stop in front of (the server asks before starting it, so a gate after it would say it ran, and the station isn't painted as waiting), except when the server asks in a stage that has already run, which then waits inside that station. The call cap and the card retry limit (with the hourly card start limit) follow the same rule: inside the station before retrying a stage that failed (an error or a usage limit, also after continuing a goal that was cut at that question), before running a block again after "Once more", and before asking task cards again for their two closing fields. The stage's status can't tell these apart (a return's stage may show done from the earlier pass or from this one, and continuing a cut goal makes the stage to do again), so the server marks such a wait (`hold.ran`); a stage stopped at an error counts as run too. Everything else is before the stage. An error, a usage limit and an agent's question wait inside the station; the goal card in front of the first station; a cut goal dashes the whole stage, and its pill shows on the cut goal's own run, or on a later run only while nothing else waits for the user (an approval, an error, a question or a pause of that run comes first); a paused run is a pill only, whose button says View. A usage-limit wait whose automatic continue was chosen is not the user's turn: it shows an hourglass and the time (24-hour, like the wait's box) instead. The gate and the pill's button scroll the chat to the box where the user answers (the approval, error or goal box in the chat, the question box above the message field, the cut notice) and put the focus on that box itself, with a visible ring, not on its first button (which could approve or accept with one Enter); if the box isn't on screen a notice says so. Hiding and showing the stage moves the focus to the button that shows or hides it again.
- **The top line**: the confirmed goal (or the request that started the run), the criteria passed and the calls used against the cap, and either the pill or the latest activity: the agent whose state changed last among those working, with the detail the server sent.
- **Motion**: the work card flying from one station to the next when the current stage changes (backwards and red for a return), under one second; the pulse of the your-turn marks (once every 1.6 seconds); and two short transitions: a station's border, shadow and background ease into a new state (0.3 seconds), and the speaker in a meeting lifts a little (0.35 seconds). With reduced motion (the app setting or the system) all of them stop; the flight is also skipped in a hidden tab. No celebration, no sound.
- **Reading it**: every state has its own shape and icon as well as a color (circle, diamond, triangle, square, dashed ring; in the strip, waiting is a taller segment with pointed ends, stopped, skipped and cut are dashed outlines, a usage limit a hatched one and an error a broken one); the stage is a named group, the pill a status, people and gates are buttons with names, and the colors are mixed from the app's theme variables, so the light and dark themes both apply (one fixed dark ink sits on the yellow, which is light in both themes). Stage names, agent names, the goal and what agents said go in as text.

No data format change. The one server change is that mark on the wait the server already sends with the run (`run.hold.ran`), added after the first release of the stage when the gate was drawn in front of stages that had already run.

## Consequences

- In the split view the user sees in one row how far the work has got, who holds it, and where it waits for them, and can jump from there to the answer. At the default heights the chat keeps more of the window than with the village (at least 180 px) and the bar's stage strip together.
- Not covered: only the latest run of the session is drawn; an `@name` run while a goal card is open shows that run (the card's pill stays).
- Not covered: the split view has no meeting-conclusion board, no sub-agent helpers and no row of agent chips (the people on the stage open the agent's panel instead); those stay in the Large office view. The chat-only strip shows the current stage only: no people, stage notes or task card titles.
- Not covered: the latest-activity line names one agent even when several work; the time left of a usage limit is recounted every 30 seconds; a hand-over between two people inside the same stage isn't animated.
- Not covered: there is no automated test of the drawing (the DOM side): layout, scrolling, focus and colors are checked by hand. The model is tested without a browser (scenes, and four runs on the fake CLI: a stage list, an approval wait, a goal card and verdict wait, a usage-limit wait), and a test checks that the class names the answer jump looks for still exist in `session.js` and `mission.js` (as text only: whether those boxes are drawn where the selectors expect is checked by hand) and that the stage uses none of them. Another test checks that the stage's classes match no unscoped rule in `app.css` or `office.css` (such as `.limit`, `.small`, `.mono` or `.gate`): the stage's state classes start with `stg-`. The fixes from a review after the screen check (the gate placement for a cap or retry limit in a stage that already ran, a cut goal's pill giving way to a later run's wait, the end flag of an unfinished run, a goal closed before its card, a stale return mark, the paused pill's button, the strip's waiting segment, the keyboard height handle and the class names) are covered by these tests only; they were not looked at on screen.
- To undo: `git revert` the commits of this change (they touch only the screen, its strings, its tests and the docs); that brings back the village in the split view with its height handle and Hide button, the "Show the office" bar and the bar's stage strip. No stored data depends on the stage.
- Screens checked in a real browser: (demo agents on the fake Claude CLI): a resting session; a stage waiting for approval with its gate and pill, and the answer button bringing the approval box into view with the focus on the box; a finished run; the folded strip and the focus following the fold and unfold buttons; the office view still showing the village and the old run bar, and coming back to the stage; a goal waiting for its card to be confirmed (gate in front, criteria count, calls); a usage-limit wait in the criteria check, as your turn and then as a plain limit wait once the automatic continue is chosen; a dark theme on a phone-width window with the current station scrolled into view; a goal with research and a meeting run to the end (a station with three people, the meeting station with its round, the opposer and the chair's line, seven stations with the past one folded and the track scrolling, criteria chips with their verdicts and the passed end flag); English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese for the resting stage and the approval wait. Not checked on screen: the card flying on a handover or a return, the return arc, an agent's question, the call cap and repeat-limit gates, a cut goal, a lost server connection, the small size under 140px, and reduced motion (tests on the model only, or not at all for the drawing).
