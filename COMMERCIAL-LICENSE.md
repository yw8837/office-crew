| Use | License | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Personal use, study, research, internal tools, open-source projects that comply with AGPL | [GNU AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE) | Free |
| Embedding in a closed-source product, offering it as a hosted/SaaS service without publishing your modified source, redistribution under other terms, or any use where AGPL obligations are not acceptable | **Commercial license** | Contact the maintainer |

Under the AGPL, if you modify Office Crew and let people use it over a network (for example a hosted service), you must make your complete modified source code available to those users under the AGPL. If you cannot or do not want to do that, you need a commercial license.

**Commercial license inquiries:** open an issue titled `[Commercial license]` at <https://github.com/yw8837/office-crew/issues> or contact the maintainer via <https://github.com/yw8837>.
