# 0028: Code notices a stalled meeting and a return that makes no progress, and a meeting can ask the user

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Related: [0009](0009-agents-ask-with-a-marked-block.md) (the question box), [0010](0010-meetings-take-turns.md) (turns and the chair's verdict), [0011](0011-meetings-pause-for-research.md), [0014](0014-goal-mode-confirmed-goal-card.md) (confirmed values become instructions), [0015](0015-goal-mode-fresh-review.md) (the criteria check and returns; not revised here), [0016](0016-goal-mode-continue-a-cut-goal.md), [0017](0017-goal-mode-research-and-meeting-first.md), [0022](0022-goal-mode-records-and-block-allow-list.md) (the block table), [0023](0023-prompt-assembly-for-cache-and-fresh-meeting-turns.md), [0025](0025-goal-report-and-evidence.md) (the goal report), `server/meeting.js` (`positionOf`, `stalled`, `parseVerdict`, `MEET_ASK_MAX`), `server/mission.js` (`sameFails`, `decidedPrompt`, `reviewPrompt`), `server/ask.js` (`answerText`), `server/orchestrator.js` (`normMeeting`, `#meeting`, `#meetingPrompt`, `#prompt`, `#decidedFor`, `#ask`, `#planStage`, `#reviewStage`, `#missionRun`, `#missionAsk`, `control`), `server/report.js`, `public/js/report-lines.js`, `public/js/stage-model.js` (`openQuestions`), `public/js/views/stage.js`, `public/js/views/session.js`, `public/js/views/mission.js` (`MissionGate`), `public/js/views/manage.js`, `test/meeting-stall.test.js`, `test/mission-review.test.js`, `test/stage-model.test.js`, `test/fake-cli/fake-claude.js`, `test/prompt-baseline.json`

## Context

A meeting ended only on the round cap, the usage cap, "End meeting" or the chair's `agree`. When the participants kept their positions, the chair judged `continue` and another round of turns ran with nothing new in it. The criteria check had the same gap: returns were limited by `returnCap`, and the same criterion failing twice added an "assumption" line, but nothing noticed that a new check failed the same criteria for the same reason. Write-ups on agent workflows built as graphs recommend two stop rules for a loop, a maximum number of passes and "no longer improving"; the app had only the first. A meeting that reached something only the user can decide (taste, scope, money, something irreversible) also had no way to ask: 0010 left "asking the user from a verdict" out and 0017 listed it as not covered.

## Options

1. A new verdict value "stalled" chosen by the chair. It costs the verdict call it is meant to save, and it is the chair's opinion.
2. The code compares facts it can read: each participant's position line between rounds, and the failed criteria and their evidence between checks. The chair's verdict gains `ask`, carried in the verdict block, and the answer becomes a confirmed instruction. A missing position line means no stall; nothing is guessed.
3. Split a stalled meeting into two options and let the user pick one (a fork card with a summary per option). Larger: summaries, a new card and new data.

## Decision

Option 2. Option 3 is not done.

- **Position line** (`positionOf`): the last line whose head is the position line of the meeting prompts in one of the four languages (`입장`, `Position`, `立场`, `立場`; a list marker, a number or bold around the head is allowed), read after NFKC normalization, with whitespace collapsed and in lower case; `null` when there is none or the value is empty. Turn prompts already asked for it in every meeting; opinion prompts asked only in goal meetings (0017). They now ask in every meeting, so round 2 can be compared with round 1 (26 characters in Korean, 68 in English per opinion prompt).
- **Stall** (`stalled`, `#meeting`): after a round of turns, at the point where the chair would judge, the code takes that round's speakers without the opposer (the chair takes no turns and is not compared). When there are two or more, each has a position line in this round and in the round before (round 2 compares with the round-1 opinions), and each one's position is unchanged, the meeting is stalled. They don't need to agree with each other. Then no verdict is called and the chair concludes with one code-written line: "Positions have stayed the same for two rounds. Decide from what has been said and write down the remaining disagreement as it is." The round cap (the last round has no verdict), the usage cap, "End meeting", stop and pause are checked first, as before. Records: `stall: { round }` on the conclusion message and on the run stage, `meetingNow.stall` during the conclusion, one chat line, and in a goal one progress-log line `kind: 'stall'` with `stall: { round }`. The chat head shows "Concluded on a stall", the flow stage's meeting station and the stage strip show "Stalled".
- **Turning it off**: `Team.stages[].meeting.stall: false`, a switch in the team's meeting settings. On by default and then not written (`normMeeting` keeps only `false`), so teams saved earlier are unchanged. A meeting can now end earlier than its rounds, so a team that wants every round can turn it off. The resume comparison (`stageDiff`) doesn't look at it: it changes how a meeting ends, not the stage list.
- **No progress** (`sameFails`, `#reviewStage`, `#missionRun`, `#missionAsk`): when a check with a verdict follows a check that had failed criteria, and the set of failed criteria and each one's evidence (NFKC, whitespace, case) are the same, the goal gets `noProgress`, the log gets `kind: 'no-progress'` (no `fail` value: the review line already counts the failure), the automatic return is not taken even when returns are left, and the verdict box (0015) shows the code's line "Another try failed the same criteria for the same reason." (`hold.noProgress`). The same happens again after "once more". `repeatFail` (the assumption line) is unchanged; no progress is narrower, since the evidence must match too. 0015 is not revised; this record points to it. With `RETURN_CAP` 1 the second check already asks, so today the visible change is the line, the log line and the report; the guard matters when more returns are left (the test raises `returnCap` to show it). The goal report counts these lines as `unverified.noProgress` ("Rounds where the same criteria failed for the same reason after another try").
- **A meeting asks the user**: the verdict block takes `{"verdict":"ask","reason":"…","question":"…","options":["…", {"label":"…","description":"…"}]}`. The question and choices are cleaned like 0009's (one line, 300, 80 and 200 characters); 2 to 4 choices are required, and a block with fewer, more or no question is a broken verdict (notice, read as `continue`). Fields in the verdict block were chosen over an `oc-ask` block after it: 0009 and 0010 read a block only as the last thing in an answer, so two closing blocks can't both count, and the block table of 0022 keeps meeting turns at `oc-verdict` and `oc-check` with no new name. The verdict prompt describes `ask` only when the run may ask (the conditions of 0009: the setting is on, not a scheduled run, not a handoff note, no session override) and questions are left (115 characters in Korean, 180 in English; `meetJudge` in `test/prompt-baseline.json` goes from 702 to 818 and from 1,371 to 1,551 for this sentence).
- **The question box**: the same box and endpoint as 0009 (numbered choices, a typed answer, skip), on the chair's verdict message (`asks[]` with `meeting: true` and the header "Meeting question"). The run stays running and the meeting waits; on the flow stage the "my turn" door is inside the meeting station and the chair waits. An answer `{ question, answer, at }` goes to `mission.decided[]` in a goal (with `stageIndex`, or `prep: true` for the pre-work meeting) with a log line marked as the user's choice (0025), otherwise to `run.stages[i].decided[]`; the question and answer are added to the verdict message as quoted lines. The meeting then goes on as after `continue` (after round 1 of a goal meeting, the turn rounds run). Skip, skipping the chair, or "End meeting" while it waits: the following turns and the conclusion of that meeting get "[Questions the user didn't answer]" with the question and "The user didn't answer. The team decides and writes down its assumption."; nothing is stored as decided (in a goal, a log line marked as the user's choice says it was skipped).
- **Where answers go** (`decidedPrompt`): a "[Decided by the user …]" block with the question, which an agent wrote, inside random markers as reference, and "User's answer: …" outside the markers as an instruction with `<` and `>` full-width, the frame of the confirmed goal block of 0014 (the goal card's own "user decides" list is shown on the card only and was never sent to agents). In a goal it goes into every later prompt of the work run: meeting turns, verdicts and conclusions, work stages, task cards, the plan and the criteria check. Outside a goal: the rest of that meeting and the later stages of the same run.
- **Cap**: `MEET_ASK_MAX` = 2 per meeting, counted from verdict messages that carry a meeting question for that stage in that run (in a goal, in the goal's work runs), so a retry, a rejected approval and a continued goal count on. Over the cap, or in a run that can't ask, `ask` is read as `continue` with a notice on the verdict message and, in a goal, a log line. A meeting of at most 3 rounds has at most two verdicts per attempt, so the cap is reached only across attempts.
- **Cut** (0016): while a goal meeting waits, `mission.meetAsk = { stageIndex, msgId, questions, round, at }` is saved. A restart cancels the open question as before (0009). When the user continues the goal, the meeting asks the same question again before round 1 without an AI call (a new entry on the same verdict message, carrying the new run's `runId` and `stageIndex`, which `openQuestions` reads first so the flow stage draws it), then runs from round 1 with the answers in `mission.decided`. Outside goal mode a run doesn't continue after a restart, as before.
- **Long sessions** (0023): the meeting engine is the same, so stall and asking work in meetings whose turns run in new conversations.

This adds `verdict: 'ask'` with `question` and `options` to `oc-verdict`, `verdict.question` and `stall` on messages, `stall` and `decided` on run stages, `stall` on `meetingNow`, `meeting`, `runId` and `stageIndex` on question entries, `stall` (only `false`) on team meeting settings, `decided`, `meetAsk` and `noProgress` on the goal, `noProgress` on the verdict hold, the log kinds `stall` and `no-progress`, and `unverified.noProgress` on the goal report. Older versions ignore them; their screen draws an `ask` verdict as "Continue" and a team's `stall: false` is dropped when they save the team.

## Not done

- Splitting a stalled meeting into two options for the user to pick.
- Whether real CLIs keep writing the position line, and how often a chair asks, is not measured; the tests use fake CLIs.
- The chair's position is not compared (it takes no turns), and neither is the opposer's. Only three-round meetings with at least four participants can stall; two-round meetings, the pre-work meeting included, never do.
- A skipped meeting question is not kept after a cut.

## Consequences

- A stalled three-round meeting of four participants saves the verdict call and the three turns of round 3.
- An answer becomes an instruction for every later step of the goal or run. It can't be taken back, only not given (skip).
- Risk: a stall can end a meeting whose next round would have changed someone's mind; the switch turns it off per team.
- Checked with fake CLIs in the tests. Screens checked in a real browser (fake Claude CLI, a temporary data folder, not port 4317), in Korean and English and at phone width: a meeting concluded on a stall ("Stalled" on the flow stage's meeting station, "Concluded on a stall" on the conclusion, the chat line), a meeting waiting on its question (the box with numbered choices, typed answer and skip; the "my turn" door inside the meeting station with the chair waiting), the verdict message after answering (the "Asked you" chip and the quoted question and answer) and after skipping, the verdict box of a goal with no progress and its progress-tab line, and the team switch saving `stall: false`. Not looked at: Simplified Chinese and Japanese (translation test only), a dark theme, a goal meeting's stall line and a re-asked question after a cut on screen (tests only). The "Meeting question" header is stored in the language in use when the question was asked, so it doesn't follow a later language switch.
- To undo: drop the stall check and the `ask` branch in `#meeting`, the `decided` block in `#prompt`, `#planStage` and `#reviewStage`, and `noProgress` in `#missionRun`; the stored fields are then unused.
