## Revision (2026-11-26, with [0030](0030-goal-run-records-retry-and-flow-map.md))

The automatic retry after a temporary outage ([0030](0030-goal-run-records-retry-and-flow-map.md)) counts as one start of a task card, so it uses up the ladder cap like an error retry, and it is not done once a card has reached its cap: the retry-limit question, with its hand-over choices, comes as before.

## Context

A team made from a document (0024) stores each agent's `tier` and, on the team, `ladder: true` and `escalateAfter: 2`, but nothing used them. `tier` is a difficulty grade across the whole team: 1 simple chores … 4 the hardest work, and several agents, also on different AIs, can share a grade. The repository owner works by a table: everyday work on the base model; core or hard work moves to a higher model only after the base model has spun twice; a truly hard problem gets the top model for one or two turns only. A task card that keeps failing went back to the same agent until the retry limit (3 starts), and then the user could only accept it or run it once more on the same agent.

## Options

1. Raise automatically after `escalateAfter` failures. Spends the more expensive model without anyone deciding; the repository owner ruled it out.
2. Ask before raising: the retry-limit question gets more choices that name higher-grade agents, and nothing changes until the user picks one.
3. Let the user change the owner by hand. The plan can't be edited (0018) and changing the team while a goal is alive is refused, so this would need a new editing path for one card.

## Decision

Option 2.

- **Ladder cap** (`ladderOf`, `cardCap`): in a team with `ladder: true` a task card may start `escalateAfter` times (default 2, 1 to 5) instead of 3; the value is set when the plan is written. What counts as a start is unchanged (0018, 0019: error retries, "once more" after a block and returns count; a retry after a usage limit doesn't). With the default the card asks before its third start, after two failures. Other teams keep 3.
- **Candidates** (`raiseTargets`): members of the task card roster of 0018 (`cardRoster`: review roles and the goal's reviewer are left out; the lead is in it, since the lead can own cards today) whose `tier` is higher than the owner's. Only the next grade up (the lowest higher grade in the team; if no member has the grade right above, the one above that). In that grade, members whose AI isn't signed in now are left out (`#usable`: the detected sign-in of the agent's AI; an agent on automatic routing needs at least one signed-in AI); the question does not move to a higher grade for that. From the rest: the first member (roster order) on the owner's AI and the first on another AI, at most two, the owner's AI first. An owner or member without a tier doesn't count; an owner on automatic routing has no AI to match and gets the first member only. One step at a time: if the card fails again with the new owner, the next question offers the grade above.
- **The choices** (`#raiseAsk`, hold `reason: 'mission', kind: 'limit'`): the hold carries `raise: [{ opt, card, from, fromName, to, name, tier, ai }]` and, when some cards of the question have no candidate, `raiseRest: [card ids]`. Choice 1 (`opt: 0`) gives each card its first candidate; choice 2 exists when some card has a second candidate (that card gets it, the others their first). The box shows them as numbered choices 3 and 4, "Hand to a higher tier — {name} ({AI · model · effort})", with "card → name" for several cards and "the rest ({ids}) once more" when `raiseRest` is set. `control` `raise` takes `to`: an agent id or `{ card id: agent id }`, which must be in the box's choices (otherwise `ok: false`). The server then works the candidates out again from the team, the agents and the sign-ins now; if a chosen pair is no longer a candidate, nothing moves, one `ask` line is logged and the same question is shown again with fresh choices, without a call. Cards of the question that aren't handed over are treated as that question's "once more" (the retry-limit question raises their cap by 1). The limit and blocked boxes also answer to number keys (as the agent question box does): 1 accept, 2 once more, 3 and 4 the hand-over choices (`gateKeys`). With no candidate at all the question is the one it was.
- **Blocked question** (`#blockedAsk`): when every blocked item is a task card, the same choices are offered for the blocked cards that have reached their start cap (another "once more" would hit the retry-limit question right away); blocked cards below the cap are in `raiseRest`. With the default `escalateAfter` of 2 a card blocked on its first start is below the cap, so a first block doesn't offer it; with `escalateAfter: 1`, or when an earlier start of the card ended in an error and was retried, the cap can already be reached at the first block. The box states the facts: "an answer says the criteria can't be met", each card's starts and cap, and that cards at their cap can be handed over.
- **Handing over** (`#raiseCard`): first the call cap (0015; "20 more" when short, and the owner stays until then). The cut point stays at the question that led there (`#capCheck` with `keepAt`), so a goal cut at that cap question keeps the answers finished in the step and, continued, asks the retry-limit or blocked question again without a call, as 0022 does for a cut blocked question. Then `ownerId` becomes the new agent, the card gets `raised: [{ from, to, at, why: 'limit'|'blocked', later? }]`, its start cap + 1 (the new owner's run), status waiting, one progress line `kind: 'raise'` written as the user's choice (`by: 'user'`, 0025), and the session is saved at once. The hourly start limit applies as before.
- **Same step**: one card per owner per step (0018). If the new owner holds another card in the card's step, the card moves to a step of its own right after that step (`later: true` on the raise; `waveStages` places it, the run stage gets `raised: true`), so it runs after the new owner's card ends. Steps moved out of the same step are ordered by when they were moved, so a step moved later goes after the ones already there and no step that may have run changes its place or name. The names are fixed by the plan: the plan's steps are "Work n/m" (m the plan's step count) and a moved step is "Work n/m ↑k1". Otherwise the card stays in its step with the new owner. Steps are always split by the owners the plan gave (`planWaves` uses the first `raised.from`), so no earlier step moves. A return re-runs the last step and the steps moved out of it (`#missionRun`, `#noFixer`), and the next check reads their answers as new ones.
- **The card's own conversation** (`#convCard`, `card.conv`): every turn of a handed-over card by its current owner (the first try, error retries, "once more", the closing-lines rewrite and returns) runs in a CLI conversation kept on the card record: `conv: { agentId, id, provider, bases, sent, noteHash }` (the conversation id and its AI, Claude's usage baseline, and what the conversation has received of the per-call head and the role notes, 0023). The agent's session conversation (`providerSessions`, `sessionProvider`, `usageBase`, `noteHashes`, `convSent`) is neither read nor written, so a lead or member who was talking with the user keeps that conversation. The answer is marked `cardConv` and, like a one-off run, counts as not being in the agent's session conversation: once the goal has ended, the agent's next message continues its session conversation and gets the card's answers as missed conversation (0023's way for long-session meetings), and the handoff note and memory rules treat them the same way. A resumed card conversation doesn't get the missed-conversation block (it got the previous conversation when it opened). Handing the card on again drops the conversation; the next owner opens its own.
- **First try and prompt** (`#raisePending`, `#raisedCard`, `#raisedBlock`): until the new owner has a finished answer for the card, each of its tries opens a new card conversation (the session's earlier conversation comes back as `[Previous conversation]`), and the card prompt gets a code-written line ("the previous owner couldn't finish this card, take it on in a new conversation") and, inside random markers labelled reference, the reason the previous owner wrote in its blocked block, if any, and the first 1,500 characters of its last answer for the card. The answer's `route.why` gets `raised` (0022); that reason alone doesn't add a `route` log line, since the `raise` line says it.
- **Cut and continue** (0016): the card (`ownerId`, `raised`, `conv`) and the run's steps are saved; continuing rebuilds the same steps from the cards, so the stage comparison is unchanged, and the past answers of a card step are collected by the card id on the message, not the step number (`pastOutputs`). A cut at the question asks it again without a call. A cut while the new owner runs a card turn drops only that card's conversation; the agent's session conversation stays. A cut during the new owner's first try restarts that try with the same block (plus the "[Continuing]" line) and doesn't count the cut start. This differs from 0018, which counts a cut start: the hand-over gives the new owner exactly one start (cap + 1), so counting a cut first try would ask the retry-limit question as soon as the goal continues, before the new owner ever finished a try (the test that continues after such a cut stops at that question when the start is counted). A new owner who left the team or now has a review role makes continuing fail with 409 by the existing rules (owner not in the card roster, role kind changed).
- **Editing**: the agent editor has "Tier (ladder)" (none, 1 to 4, with the hint that it is a difficulty grade across the team), the team editor a "Ladder team" switch with "Ask after this many failures" (1 to 5, default 2), so teams not made from a document can use this. `cleanAgent` keeps `tier` (rounded and clamped to 1 to 4; empty or not a number removes it; a request without the field keeps the saved value); `cleanTeam` keeps `ladder: true` with `escalateAfter` (clamped to 1 to 5, default 2) and removes both otherwise. The team draft prompt of 0024 says what `tier` means.
- **Screen**: the plan card and the progress tab show "↑ {previous} → {new}" on a raised card; the flow stage's gate keeps the kinds `limit` and `blocked` and adds "can be handed to {names} (higher tier)" to its text. The goal report (0025) lists the handed cards with who they went from and to (`raised: [{ id, chain }]`, from the card records), and its "what you confirmed" part has the `raise` lines.
- **Estimate** (0019): the call estimate doesn't include calls from raising; for a ladder team the goal card says so in one line. The estimate after the plan (0025) is made before any card runs, so no step has been moved yet.

This adds `raised` and `conv` to task cards, `raised: true` to run stages, `raise` (with `opt`) and `raiseRest` to the limit and blocked holds, the `control` action `raise` with `to`, the log kind `raise`, the route reason `raised`, `cardConv` on answers, `raised` on report messages, and edited `tier`, `ladder` and `escalateAfter`. Older versions ignore them; a cut goal whose card was moved to a step of its own fails the stage comparison there (409) and can only be discarded with an older version.

## Relation to 0014

0014 keeps each agent's own model and has no router that picks a model by how hard a task looks. That still holds: nothing reads the task to pick an agent or a model, the plan assigns owners as before, and a higher grade works on a card only after the card failed and the user chose to hand it over.

## Relation to 0018

0018 already has two revisions (0022, 0025), so it gets no revision section for this; this record is where the changes to it are. For a ladder team: the retry limit is `escalateAfter` instead of 3; the retry-limit question and the blocked question can offer handing cards to a higher-grade member; a card handed to an owner who already holds a card in its step runs in a step of its own right after that step ("Work n/m ↑k1"), and steps are split by the owners the plan gave; card records gain `raised` and `conv`, run stages `raised: true`; a cut first try of a new owner is not counted. Everything else in 0018 holds as written.

## Not done

- Raising in stage-list mode (the team's own stages): only task cards.
- Picking the owner by difficulty from the start, or lowering a card to a lower grade.
- More than two choices per question, or a member of a grade other than the next one up.

## Consequences

- A raise costs at least one call on the higher grade, plus whatever that owner's retries cost; the estimate doesn't show it.
- The lead is a candidate when it has a higher tier, because the current owner rule lets the lead own cards; its conversation with the user is kept, since the card runs in a conversation of its own.
- Not covered: when a card is handed over inside its step (not moved to a step of its own), the changed-files check of 0025 still compares with the state before the step, so a file the previous owner changed in that step counts as changed for the new owner's claim. A card moved to a step of its own is checked against the state before that step (tested).
- Not covered: how real CLIs do on a raised card is not measured; the tests use the fake Claude CLI (`FAILBY:<name>`, `BLOCKBY:<name>`, `SLOWBY:<name>` in the goal sentence: that agent's cards fail, end blocked, or answer 3 seconds late; `<name>@k1` limits it to one card; `PLAN3` plans three cards in one step, `PLANBUILD1` makes k1 a build card, `NOWRITEK1BY:<name>` makes that agent's k1 claim a file without writing it).
- Screens checked in a real browser (the fake Claude CLI on a temporary app and data folder): the blocked question with the hand-over choice, picked with the number key 3 (the card stayed in its step with the new owner); the retry-limit question with the choice, picked by clicking, where the new owner already had a card in the same step (the step split into "Work 1/2" and "Work 2/2", the names used before the fixed names below); the flow stage's gate text; "↑ previous → new" on the plan card and in the progress tab, and the `raise` log line; the agent editor's tier (set to 3, then cleared, and the stored value read back) and the team editor's ladder switch and count (set to 3, then off, read back); the goal card's ladder line under the estimate; English for the retry-limit question, the gate and the plan card; a 375 px window. Not checked on screen: Simplified Chinese and Japanese (translation test only), the dark theme, continuing a cut goal, a return that runs a moved step, and the call-cap question before handing over (tests only). After the review fixes (Korean, desktop width, a temporary app on the fake CLIs): the retry-limit question with two hand-over choices (a Claude and a Codex member of the next grade), picked with the number key 3; the choice "k1 → name · the rest (k2) once more" at a return, picked by clicking; the blocked question's new wording with "(2 starts · limit 2)" and its hand-over choice, picked by clicking (the card moved to "Work 1/1 ↑k1"); the step names "Work 1/1" and "Work 1/1 ↑k1" on the flow stage; the goal report's "Cards handed to a higher tier" section and the hand-over line under what you confirmed. Not checked on screen after the fixes: English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese (translation test only), a phone-width window and the dark theme.
- To undo: stop putting `raise` on the holds (the `raise` action is then refused) and plan with `CARD_TRIES` again; stored `raised` cards and `raised` steps still continue correctly, and a stored `conv` is then unused.
