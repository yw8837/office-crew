# 0027: Goal mode asks before handing a stuck task card to a higher-tier agent

## Context

A team made from a document (0024) stores each agent's `tier` (1 the cheapest and simplest work, 4 the most expensive and hardest) and, on the team, `ladder: true` and `escalateAfter: 2`, but nothing used them. The repository owner works by a table: everyday work on the base model; core or hard work moves to a higher model only after the base model has spun twice; a truly hard problem gets the top model for one or two turns only. A task card that keeps failing went back to the same agent until the retry limit (3 starts), and then the user could only accept it or run it once more on the same agent.

## Options

1. Raise automatically after `escalateAfter` failures. Spends the more expensive model without anyone deciding; the repository owner ruled it out.
2. Ask before raising: the retry-limit question gets one more choice that names the higher-tier agent, and nothing changes until the user picks it.
3. Let the user change the owner by hand. The plan can't be edited (0018) and changing the team while a goal is alive is refused, so this would need a new editing path for one card.

## Decision

Option 2.

- **Ladder cap** (`ladderOf`, `cardCap`): in a team with `ladder: true` a task card may start `escalateAfter` times (default 2, 1 to 5) instead of 3; the value is set when the plan is written. What counts as a start is unchanged (0018, 0019: error retries, "once more" after a block and returns count; a retry after a usage limit doesn't). With the default the card asks before its third start, after two failures. Other teams keep 3.
- **The choice** (`#cardGate`, hold `reason: 'mission', kind: 'limit'`): when every card in the question has a candidate, the hold carries `raise: [{ card, from, fromName, to, name, tier, ai }]` and the box shows a third numbered choice, "Hand to a higher tier — {name} ({AI · model · effort})". The limit and blocked boxes also answer to number keys (as the agent question box does). Candidate (`raiseTarget`): a member of the task card roster of 0018 (`cardRoster`: review roles and the goal's reviewer are left out; the lead is in it, since the lead can own cards today) whose `tier` is higher than the owner's. Only the next tier up (the lowest higher tier in the team), the owner's provider first, then roster order. An owner or member without a tier doesn't count; an owner on automatic routing has no provider to match. One step at a time: if the card fails again with the new owner, the next question offers the tier above. With no candidate (the owner is the top tier, nobody is higher, not a ladder team, or any card of the question has none) the question is the one it was.
- **Blocked question** (`#blockedAsk`): the same choice appears only when every blocked item is a task card that has reached its start cap (another "once more" would hit the retry-limit question right away). A first block, one failure, doesn't offer it: a block is the agent saying it can't, which makes raising a natural answer, but the owner's rule is "only after two failures".
- **Handing over** (`#raiseCard`): first the call cap (0015; "20 more" when short, and the owner stays until then). Then `ownerId` becomes the new agent, the card gets `raised: [{ from, to, at, why: 'limit'|'blocked', later? }]`, its start cap + 1 (the new owner's run), status waiting, one progress line `kind: 'raise'`, and the session is saved at once. The hourly start limit applies as before.
- **Same step**: one card per owner per step (0018). If the new owner holds another card in the card's step, the card moves to a step of its own right after that step (`later: true` on the raise; `waveStages` places it, the run stage gets `raised: true`), so it runs after the new owner's card ends. Otherwise the card stays in its step with the new owner. Steps are always split by the owners the plan gave (`planWaves` uses the first `raised.from`), so no earlier step moves. A return re-runs the last step and the steps moved out of it (`#missionRun`, `#noFixer`), and the next check reads their answers as new ones.
- **New conversation and prompt** (`#raisePending`, `#raisedCard`, `#raisedBlock`): until the new owner has a finished answer for the card, its turn on the card drops its saved CLI conversation (like a cut agent in 0016; the earlier conversation comes back as `[Previous conversation]`) and the card prompt gets a code-written line ("the previous owner couldn't finish this card, take it on in a new conversation") and, inside random markers labelled reference, the reason the previous owner wrote in its blocked block, if any, and the first 1,500 characters of its last answer for the card. The answer's `route.why` gets `raised` (0022); that reason alone doesn't add a `route` log line, since the `raise` line says it.
- **Cut and continue** (0016): the card (`ownerId`, `raised`) and the run's steps are saved; continuing rebuilds the same steps from the cards, so the stage comparison is unchanged. A cut at the question asks it again without a call. A cut while the new owner runs its first try restarts that try in a new conversation with the same block (plus the "[Continuing]" line) and doesn't count the cut start. A new owner who left the team or now has a review role makes continuing fail with 409 by the existing rules (owner not in the card roster, role kind changed).
- **Editing**: the agent editor has "Tier (ladder)" (none, 1 to 4), the team editor a "Ladder team" switch with "Ask after this many failures" (1 to 5, default 2), so teams not made from a document can use this. `cleanAgent` keeps `tier` (rounded and clamped to 1 to 4; empty or not a number removes it; a request without the field keeps the saved value); `cleanTeam` keeps `ladder: true` with `escalateAfter` (clamped to 1 to 5, default 2) and removes both otherwise.
- **Screen**: the plan card and the progress tab show "↑ {previous} → {new}" on a raised card; the flow stage's gate keeps the kinds `limit` and `blocked` and adds "can be handed to {name} (higher tier)" to its text.
- **Estimate** (0019): the call estimate doesn't include calls from raising; for a ladder team the goal card says so in one line.

This adds `raised` to task cards, `raised: true` to run stages, `raise` to the limit and blocked holds, the `control` action `raise`, the log kind `raise`, the route reason `raised`, and edited `tier`, `ladder` and `escalateAfter`. Older versions ignore them; a cut goal whose card was moved to a step of its own fails the stage comparison there (409) and can only be discarded with an older version.

## Relation to 0014

0014 keeps each agent's own model and has no router that picks a model by how hard a task looks. That still holds: nothing reads the task to pick an agent or a model, the plan assigns owners as before, and a higher tier works on a card only after the card failed and the user chose to hand it over.

## Not done

- Raising in stage-list mode (the team's own stages): only task cards.
- Picking the owner by difficulty from the start, or lowering a card to a lower tier.
- Choosing among several candidates or raising some cards of one question and not others: the question offers the one candidate per card the rule picks, for all its cards at once.

## Consequences

- A raise costs at least one call on the higher tier, plus whatever that owner's retries cost; the estimate doesn't show it.
- Not covered: the lead is a candidate when it has a higher tier, because the current owner rule lets the lead own cards; a team whose core agent is also the lead would otherwise have no candidate.
- Not covered: how real CLIs do on a raised card is not measured; the tests use the fake Claude CLI (`FAILBY:<name>`, `BLOCKBY:<name>`, `SLOWBY:<name>` in the goal sentence: that agent's cards fail, end blocked, or answer 3 seconds late).
- Screens checked in a real browser (the fake Claude CLI on a temporary app and data folder): the blocked question with the hand-over choice, picked with the number key 3 (the card stayed in its step with the new owner); the retry-limit question with the choice, picked by clicking, where the new owner already had a card in the same step (the step split into "Work 1/2" and "Work 2/2"); the flow stage's gate text; "↑ previous → new" on the plan card and in the progress tab, and the `raise` log line; the agent editor's tier (set to 3, then cleared, and the stored value read back) and the team editor's ladder switch and count (set to 3, then off, read back); the goal card's ladder line under the estimate; English for the retry-limit question, the gate and the plan card; a 375 px window. Not checked on screen: Simplified Chinese and Japanese (translation test only), the dark theme, continuing a cut goal, a return that runs a moved step, and the call-cap question before handing over (tests only).
- To undo: stop putting `raise` on the holds (the `raise` action is then refused) and plan with `CARD_TRIES` again; stored `raised` cards and `raised` steps still continue correctly.
