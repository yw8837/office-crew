# 0019: Goal mode shows a rough call estimate before confirming, and waits for a usage-limit reset when no other AI can take over

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Related: [0014](0014-goal-mode-confirmed-goal-card.md) (the goal card), [0015](0015-goal-mode-fresh-review.md) (the criteria check, returns, the call cap), [0016](0016-goal-mode-continue-a-cut-goal.md) (the cut point and continuing), [0017](0017-goal-mode-research-and-meeting-first.md) (the research and meeting estimate on the card), [0018](0018-goal-mode-task-cards.md) (task cards), `server/mission.js` (`estimateParts`, `estimateCalls`, `resumePoint`, `QUOTA_AUTO`, `QUOTA_SLACK`), `public/js/mission-numbers.js` (`sumEstimate`, `limitRows`), `server/orchestrator.js` (`#goalTurn`, `confirmMission`, `resumeMission`, `#runStage`, `#missionRun`, `#reviewStage`, `#quotaUntil`, `#quotaAsk`, `#hold`, `control`), `server/failover.js` (`Cooldown`), `server/store.js` (`attentionOf`), `server/notify.js`, `public/js/views/mission.js` (`EstimateLine`, `LimitLine`, `GoalCard`, `MissionGate`, `CutBox`), `public/js/views/session.js`, `public/js/main.js`, `public/js/state.js`, `test/mission-estimate.test.js`, `test/fake-cli/fake-claude.js`

## Context

Before confirming a goal the user saw an estimate for research and the pre-work meeting only (0017); the whole goal had no figure until the call cap of 40 stopped it midway (0015). The subscription percentages the app already receives were shown in the usage panel, away from the goal. And when a stage of a goal hit a usage limit with no other AI to take over, the work stage fell into the generic error wait and the criteria check ended with no verdict; the user had to notice, come back after the reset and press retry.

## Options

1. Predict the cost in tokens or in subscription percent. Tokens per call vary by a wide margin, plans differ, and nothing has been measured; a number like that would look exact and be wrong.
2. A call range worked out only from what the code already knows (who runs, how many steps, the existing estimates), the percentages the app already holds shown as they are, and a dedicated wait for usage limits that can continue by itself at a reset time it actually knows.
3. Retry automatically with a backoff whenever a limit is hit. Spends calls with nobody watching, and the reset time is often unknown.

## Decision

Option 2.

- **Estimate parts** (`estimateParts`, put on the goal card as `estimate`): the calls already used to set the goal (`spent`), the cap, research (min 3, max 8: the scan, the questions, 1 to 6 questions, from `prepCard`) and the pre-work meeting (min: the count when everyone agrees in round 1, max: the meeting's call count plus up to 3 research turns, from `prepCard`) with whether the team can do them, the criteria check (1 when there is a reviewer), automatic returns (`RETURN_CAP` when there is a reviewer, else 0), task cards (plan 1 + 1 to `CARD_COUNT` cards; a return re-runs at most one card per person of the card roster) or `null` when the team can't use cards, and one entry per team stage: its calls (the number of agents; a meeting stage `meetingCallsMin` to `meetingCalls`), whether it is a review stage, whether it can be left out for the criteria check, and what a return to it costs (the number of agents; a meeting stage 1, the lead alone).
- **One sum, shared** (`sumEstimate` in `public/js/mission-numbers.js`; `server/mission.js` imports it, as the server already imports the UI dictionaries): with the user's choice (way of working, research, meeting, kept review stages) it adds the chosen parts; the stages left are the ones that can't be left out plus the ones kept; the return stage is the last of them that isn't a review stage, else the last (the rule of `reviewPlan`); max = min + returns × (return stage + check). The screen recalculates as the boxes change; when a confirm gets 409 because the team changed (review, prep or work), the answer also carries the current team's `estimate` parts and the line is summed again from them; confirming stores `estimateCalls` of the confirmed choice as `mission.estimate` (`{ min, max, returns, cap, spent }`). The card says "about a to b calls (including the s already used to set the goal and up to n automatic return) · cap 40", that this is a rough figure and not a forecast, that re-asks, retries, takeovers and retries after a limit cost more, and, when max passes the cap, that it will ask at the cap.
- **Actual calls**: a confirmed card whose goal ended `done`, `accepted` or `stopped` shows "actual calls k (estimate a to b)"; a goal without `estimate` (older data) shows the actual count only.
- **Limit line**: the goal card carries `providers` (the AI each team member is set to; agents on automatic routing are left out because the AI isn't known). While the card is open, and while the goal is alive, the card lists for those providers the windows in the limit record the screen already has (`state.usage.limits`, filled by the app's earlier runs) whose percentage is a number and whose reset time is in the future or absent, with the reset time and how old the record is (`limitRows`, `recordAge`: minutes under an hour, hours under a day, days beyond, and over a day it is marked as an old record). Window names are the ones of the usage panel (5-hour, weekly, primary, secondary; other keys as they come). With no such window the line isn't drawn. Nothing is read anew and no percentage is estimated.
- **Usage-limit wait** (`#quotaAsk`): in a goal's work run, when a work stage (a team stage or a task card step; not research, the questions, the plan, the pre-work meeting or a team meeting stage) fails and every failed person's error is a usage limit (`isQuotaError`, after `#failover` found no AI to take over, or takeover is off), or the criteria check ends with a usage limit the same way, the run asks with `hold.reason: 'quota'` instead of the error wait or "no verdict". The goal is `waiting`; the reset time (`#quotaUntil`) is, per person, taken for the AI that actually ran last and hit the limit (after a takeover, the last AI; not the AI the agent is set to): the later of a window in its limit record that is really full (`limitResetAt`: 100% or more, or a window of a rejected record without a percentage; not the 95% takeover threshold, so 5-hour 100% and weekly 96% gives the 5-hour reset) and a time in the error text (`resetFromText`), else a rest mark set from an error text (`Cooldown` now records `source`; the 30-minute guess and marks from the 95% threshold don't count); over people the latest; unknown if anyone's is unknown. That AI is saved with each person in the cut point. The choices, in this order: "Continue after HH:MM (automatic)" (only with a known time) · "Retry now" · **Skip** in a work stage or **Accept** in the criteria check (the exits the error wait and the no-verdict question had) · "Drop it"; with no known time the box says to press Retry now when the limit lifts. The AI's error message is shown in the box only.
- **Skip and Accept**: Skip in a work stage does what Skip did in the error wait: the stage is marked skipped and the run goes on with the answers it has; in a task card step the cards that hit the limit are recorded as gaps (`by: 'quota'`), like an accepted block: later cards are told there is no result and the criteria check gets a code-written line saying the result is empty. Accept in the criteria check does what accepting after "no verdict" did: the round is recorded without a verdict (every criterion `unknown`) and the goal ends `accepted` without asking again.
- **Automatic continue**: `later` (a new `control` action) starts a timer in memory for the reset time + `QUOTA_SLACK` (45 seconds, `quotaDelay`); the box is shown again with the same `hold.at` (no second notification) and still takes the other choices. When it fires and pause was pressed meanwhile, nothing starts: the run waits paused and continues on resume. It can be used `QUOTA_AUTO` (3) times per goal (`mission.quota.autos`, counted only when the step really starts again); after that it isn't offered. The timer is cleared when the wait ends (any choice, stop, deleting the session, closing the app). `OFFICE_CREW_QUOTA_RESUME_MS` replaces the delay only while `OFFICE_CREW_FAKE_ONLY` is on (tests; the reset time shown is unchanged).
- **What a retry runs**: before any retry (Retry now or automatic, work stage, task card step or check) the call cap of 0015 is checked for the people that will run again; when it is short the run first asks "20 more?" (the finished answers stay in the cut point). Then, in a work stage, only the people that hit the limit run again and finished answers are kept; a task card re-run for a limit continues the same start and isn't counted in the card's starts or the hourly start rate of 0018. In the check, the same round again from the start; the answer cut by the limit is marked (`review.quota`) and shown as "stopped at a limit" instead of a second answer of the same round. Extra instructions sent during the wait go into the re-run. Log lines: the stop (`kind: 'quota'`, `fail: 'outside'`), the choice, each automatic start ("n of 3"). Choosing Drop it also clears the office marks, as in the error wait.
- **Cut and continue** (0016): the cut point records `wait: { reason: 'quota', until, failed: [{ agentId, error }] }` (finished answers stay in `finished`). After a restart the timer is gone; the cut notice shows "limit due to reset at HH:MM" (or that it passed, or that it was unknown), and **Continue** shows the same wait again without an AI call, with the saved reset time (the "in n hours" of an error text isn't read again). When that time has passed and nothing newer is known, the box says the reset time passed and to press Retry now (no automatic choice).
- The sidebar, the "other session" notice and phone notifications name the wait as its own kind (`quota`); the stage strip marks the stage ⏳.
- Unchanged: the call cap and "20 more" (0015). A stage isn't re-run on a bigger model.

This adds `estimate` and `providers` to goal cards (and `estimate` to the 409 body), `estimate` and `quota` to `mission`, the `quota` wait to `at.wait` and to run holds, `later` and the quota uses of `skip`, `accept`, `retry` for `control`, `by: 'quota'` on task cards, `review.quota` on review answers, the `quota` attention kind and `source` on rest marks. Older versions ignore the new fields; a cut goal whose cut point is a usage-limit wait can't be continued by an older version (it reads no cut point) and can only be discarded there.

## Consequences

- A user sees a range for the whole goal before confirming, and the actual count next to it afterwards, so the estimate can be compared over time. A limit with no takeover no longer needs the user to come back at the right time when the reset time is known.
- Not covered: percentages come only from records the app already received (from earlier runs); they can be old (the line says how old), and how much of a window this goal will use isn't estimated or converted.
- Not covered: the limit message and reset-time formats of the real CLIs were not checked with a real CLI in this change; the tests use the fake CLI, and the reading is what `server/failover.js` already did.
- Not covered: the accuracy of the estimate isn't measured beyond the fake CLI runs in the tests (task cards with research and a meeting, the stage list with a return, the stage list with research all landed inside the range). It leaves out block and empty-answer re-asks, error retries, takeovers, closing-line retries of task cards, research turns inside a team meeting stage, "once more" chosen by the user, and retries after a limit.
- Not covered: a usage limit in the scan, the research questions, a research question, the plan, the pre-work meeting, a team meeting stage (also its lead-alone return), the goal turn or a task card's closing-line retry keeps its earlier handling (the scan is skipped, the questions fall back to one question, a research branch is "not checked", the plan falls back to one card, a meeting stage gets the error wait, the goal turn closes the goal, the card becomes a gap). A stage where someone failed for another reason gets the error wait.
- Not covered: the automatic continue timer doesn't survive closing the app; the times shown and logged use this PC's clock.
- To undo: drop the quota branch in `#runStage` and `#reviewStage` (limits fall back to the error wait and "no verdict"), and stop drawing `EstimateLine` and `LimitLine`; the stored fields are then unused.
- Screens checked in a real browser (demo agents on the fake Claude CLI): the estimate range on the goal card and its redraw when the way of working changes; the limit line after a limit was hit; the usage-limit wait in a work stage (reset time, automatic continue, retry now, skip, drop) and skipping to the next stage; the wait in the verdict with "accept"; the actual-calls line after a stop and after accepting; a dark theme on a phone-width window; English for the estimate, the limit line, the wait and the actual-calls line; Simplified Chinese and Japanese for the estimate, the limit line and the wait in a work stage. Not checked on screen: the automatic continue firing at the reset time, a wait with no known reset time, a wait inside task cards, the call-cap question before a retry, and picking a wait up again after restarting the app (tests only).
