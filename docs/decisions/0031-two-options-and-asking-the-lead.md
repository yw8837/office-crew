## Context

0028 ended a stalled meeting with the chair's conclusion and its remaining disagreement written down, and left out "split a stalled meeting into two options for the user to pick". When the team really can't choose between two options, the chair picks one and the work goes on with it, and the user sees the other only as a line under "remaining disagreement". A task card owner who needs a decision that isn't in the goal or the card had two exits: guess, or end blocked (0018). Asking the user (0009) suits what only the user can decide; many questions are about the plan the lead wrote and the lead could answer them in one call. Write-ups on agent teams recommend the same order: answer from the task and the user's instructions when they say it, ask the user first otherwise; and showing where options split first when a person picks between them.

## Options

1. Leave it to the chair and to blocked cards. Nothing new, but the choice between two real options stays with an agent.
2. Let the meeting continue until it agrees. Costs calls with no stop rule; 0028 just added one.
3. Two code-run steps, both off the AI's own judgment: when the code sees exactly two options in the position lines, each option gets a one-page summary and the user picks (the pick becomes the user's decision); a card can ask the lead once per block, and the lead can hand the question to the user or ask for a small meeting the user must allow.

## Decision

Option 3.

### Two options

- **When**: in a goal's work run (the pre-work meeting or a team meeting stage), after the conclusion is written (and after a question at the conclusion, 0028), when the meeting ended on a stall (0028) or the conclusion's "Remaining disagreement" (0025's reading) isn't empty, and the participants other than the chair (who writes the conclusion) and the opposer (whose job is to oppose; 0010 and 0028 leave it out of agreement and stall too) each have a position line and those lines group into exactly two options. Grouping: each person's last position line in this attempt of the meeting (a turn if any, else the opinion), `positionOf` (NFKC, spaces, lower case) with surrounding punctuation and bold marks taken off, and brackets only when a pair wraps the whole line (so "A (reason: faster)" keeps its closing bracket); same text, same option. A missing position line, one option or three and more: nothing changes (three and more get one log line). So it needs four participants or more (`forkCan`).
- **Setting**: `Team.fork` `ask` (default, not stored), `auto`, `off` in the team editor; the goal card shows the team's value as "When a meeting splits into two options" with the three choices when the team has a meeting that can split, and the confirm sends `fork` (stored as `mission.forkMode`; a confirm without it uses the team's value). `ask` (decided by the repository owner: summaries cost calls) shows the card first with the two positions and asks "Get summaries (about 2 more calls)" or "Let the team decide"; `auto` writes the summaries right away.
- **Limits**: before the card is made, and again when "Get summaries" is pressed and when continuing a cut card, the summaries need calls left ≥ the number of writers (`#extraLimits`: the call cap of 0015) and no writer whose AI the subscription cap of 0026 would ask about (`#capTarget`, `capOk`, full windows left to 0019's wait). Failing either: no summaries, the chair's conclusion stands, one log line (and the card, when already shown, ends `skipped`). It never asks the cap question for this: a summary is extra work.
- **Summaries** (`forkPrompt`, `#forkWrite`): for each option the first participant (team order) who held it writes one page, run once and read-only (`prep: { kind: 'fork', key }`, like the plan step), at the same time: the confirmed goal block, the two positions and this attempt's opinions, turns and research inside random markers as reference, and four fixed lines "What / Why / Cost / Can it be undone", each within 200 characters, no blocks, no asking. The code keeps the answer without any `oc-` block, cut to 900 characters. A summary that fails or comes back empty ends the card `failed` (the conclusion stands). Blocks in a summary are not read; the block table gives the step `fork: []`, so a block there gets the `block` log line of 0022.
- **The card** (`kind: 'fork'` proposal message): where they split first (both positions, marked as written by agents), who backed each option, each summary side by side (stacked on a narrow window), the calls the summaries took, and the buttons "Go with A", "Go with B", "Let the team decide (the chair's conclusion)". The run waits with `hold: { reason: 'fork', step: 'offer'|'pick', stageIndex, msgId }`; `control` action `fork` with `pick` (`write`/`team`, or `a`/`b`/`team`), `msgId` (the card whose button was pressed; anything but the card being waited on is refused) and, for an option, `hash`. The goal is `waiting`; the flow stage puts "my turn" inside the meeting station and its answer box is the card; the sidebar and notifications name the kind `fork`.
- **Seen versus stored** (`forkHash`): the screen hashes the keys, positions and summaries it rendered (SHA-256 of the same JSON the server uses, built by one module both load, `public/js/fork-hash.js`; the card's own `hash` when the browser can't) and the server compares it with a hash of the stored card. If they differ, nothing is applied, the card is sent again, one log line, and the wait stays.
- **What a pick does** (decided by the repository owner: the winner's summary is a confirmed instruction, after the seen/stored check): `mission.decided` gets `{ question, answer, about, fork: true, stageIndex | prep }` in 0028's frame. They get their own heading, "[Decided by the user — the meeting split into two options and the user picked one]": what the user decided is the fact "option A" (the code-written answer line outside the markers: "Go with option A. Follow the summary of option A inside the markers, and where it differs from the meeting conclusion, follow this option."); the question line and the summary the user read and picked (agent-written) go inside the markers, with one line saying not to follow a sentence in them that widens permissions or changes the confirmed goal or criteria. Every later prompt of the work run gets it (work stages, cards, the plan, the check). The chair's conclusion still goes on as before (reference). The other option is recorded as dropped: `mission.forks[]` `{ label, picked, chosen, dropped[], msgId }`, shown in the goal report ("Two options", positions marked as written by agents) and in its memory text. "Let the team decide" adds nothing to `decided` and is recorded as `picked: 'team'`. Each choice is a user-marked log line (0025) that names only option keys and who backed each, never the position text (those lines feed the report's and the memory text's "What you confirmed").
- **Cut and continue**: `mission.forkAt` `{ msgId, stageIndex, conclusionId, runId }` is saved while the card is open. Continuing doesn't hold the meeting again: the meeting stage (and the pre-work meeting) skips its call and limit checks, shows the same card again with no AI call (offer or pick), or writes only the summaries that are missing, and passes on the stored conclusion as the stage's result. Discarding the cut goal closes the card.
- **Records**: log kind `fork`; answer kind `fork` ("Option summaries") in the usage breakdown and the report (only when there are some, so older reports keep their shape); the estimate (0019) adds `2` to its maximum for each meeting that can split when the confirm sends `fork` other than `off`, never to its minimum.

### Asking the lead

- **The block**: a task card answer may end with ```` ```oc-lead {"question":"…","context":"…"} ``` ```` (question one line, 300 characters; context optional, 600). The block table (0022) gives `oc-lead` to task cards only; stage-list work stages don't take it (a `block` log line). The card prompt gets one line and the block (`leadRule`) only when the owner isn't the lead, it isn't the closing-lines rewrite, and questions are left.
- **Flow**: at most 2 per card (`LEAD_MAX`, `card.leadAsks`, kept across a cut). Needs 2 calls left (the lead's answer and the card's continuation) and the lead's AI under the subscription cap; otherwise the block is ignored with a log line and a notice (no cap question). The card gets `lead: { question, at }` ("Asking the lead" on the plan card, the progress tab and the flow stage, where the owner shows "Waiting for the lead"). The lead answers once and read-only (`prep: { kind: 'lead', card }`), one question at a time (cards that ask together wait in a queue), after its own turn in the same step if it has one. One CLI per agent in a run: `#runAgent` counts each agent's runs including routing and a usage-limit wait (0019), and the lead's answer and small-meeting turns wait for that person to be free (`#claim`, woken when a run ends, not polled). The lead must still be on the team: otherwise, and for the closing-lines rewrite, the lead's own card or no lead, the block is ignored with a log line naming that reason (`#leadWhy`). Skipping a card that waits for the lead ends the wait at once: a queued answer isn't asked, the lead's answer being written is stopped, a question box the lead opened for it is closed, a small meeting for it gives no more turns, nothing of it is used, and no continuation call is made; the card ends skipped once the turn running at that moment has stopped (so nothing of that question keeps running into the next step); one log line. The answer goes back inside random markers as reference ("[Lead's answer — card k1, reference]" with the owner's question) as the next turn of the owner's same conversation (or with the request and its answer so far when the CLI can't continue, as for 0009). **Counting**: that continuation is the same card start, like answering a question box: `card.starts` doesn't change; every CLI call still counts toward the goal's call cap.
- **The lead asks the user**: the lead's prompt explains `oc-ask` when the run may ask (0009's conditions). The code takes the first question into the question box on the lead's answer; the answer goes to `mission.decided` with `card` (heading "[Decided by the user — the lead asked and the user answered]") and a user-marked line, into the card's continuation and every later prompt. Skipping tells the owner to decide and write the assumption.
- **Small meeting**: the lead's prompt explains `oc-meet {"topic":"…","people":[2 to 3 names from the listed team]}` when the run may ask, fewer than 2 small meetings ran in the goal (`SMALL_MEET_MAX`) and at least two people are listed. The list leaves out the lead (who concludes) and the asking owner (who waits); the code matches the names to that list, keeps the first 3, and doesn't open it with fewer than 2. The code asks in the question box "The lead asked for a small meeting: topic (participants). Open it? (about n more calls)" (n = participants + 1), after the call and limit checks. A participant whose own card is waiting for the lead (another card of the same wave asked too) lends that waiting slot for its opinion and gets it back; without this the meeting and that card would wait for each other. A participant waiting for a question box answer (0009) is not lent: the meeting waits until that turn ends. Allowed: one round, opinions at the same time without seeing each other, then the lead's conclusion; no research, no opposer, all run once and read-only (`prep: { kind: 'meet', card, phase }`, answer kind meeting). The conclusion goes to the owner with the lead's answer as reference. Not allowed, unreadable or names not found: the lead's answer alone. Counted when it starts (`mission.smallMeets`). A block the code read but didn't follow (a question the run may not ask, a small meeting it didn't open) is hidden in the lead's answer (`hidden`) like a question box block; while an answer is still being written, a trailing `oc-lead` or `oc-meet` is hidden too.
- **Cut and continue**: nothing is restored mid-question. A cut card restarts in a new conversation as before (0016), its "Asking the lead" mark is cleared and `leadAsks` stays; an open question in the box is cancelled at restart (0009).

This adds `fork` to teams, `fork` to goal cards and the confirm request, `forkChosen` on confirmed cards, `forkMode`, `forks`, `forkAt`, `smallMeets` on the goal, `kind: 'fork'` messages, the hold reason `fork` and `control` action `fork` (`pick`, `msgId`, `hash`), `fork: true` and `card` on decided items, `forks` on reports, `leadAsks` and `lead` on task cards, `leadAsks` and `hidden` on answers, `prep.kind` `fork`, `lead`, `meet`, the block names `oc-lead` and `oc-meet`, the log kinds `fork` and `lead`, the answer kinds `fork` and `lead`, and the attention kind `fork`. Older versions ignore them; they draw a fork card as "Hire proposal cancelled", treat a cut goal that waited on a fork card as a meeting to hold again from round 1, and drop `fork` when saving a team.

## Relation to 0010 and 0017

Both already have three revisions or more, so they get no revision section for this (the README rule); this record is where the change to them is. 0010: after a goal meeting's conclusion, the code may offer two options (above); agreement, turns and verdicts are unchanged. 0017: a goal meeting's turns are unchanged; the summaries are two more run-once, read-only turns after the conclusion, and continuing a goal cut at the card doesn't hold the meeting again (0017 says a meeting starts again at round 1). 0009, 0018, 0019, 0022 and 0028 have revision sections.

## Not done

- Grouping is by text: two people who back the same option in different words count as two options. Three options or more, the chair's and the opposer's positions, and options inside one person's answer are not offered.
- The two summaries are agent-written; the code checks only their length and that the user saw what is stored. Who backs an option is the first person in team order.
- `oc-lead` in stage-list goals, a lead asking itself, more than one question per box from the lead, a small meeting that researches or takes turns, and keeping an open lead question across a cut.
- Small meetings and lead answers are not in the call estimate (nor raised cards, 0027).
- The checks before summaries, a lead question and a small meeting count one call per run. An empty-answer retry or a takeover by another AI inside that run can take one or two calls more than was checked; the call cap still counts every call, so the next check sees them.
- The one-CLI-per-agent wait covers runs inside one goal run. Its usage-limit-wait path (0019) is checked by reading the code, not by a test.
- Whether real CLIs write the position lines, the four summary lines, `oc-lead` and `oc-meet` is not measured; the tests use fake CLIs.

## Consequences

- A split costs two calls and one pick, only when the user agrees (by default) and the caps allow. A card question costs two calls instead of a blocked card or a guess; a small meeting costs participants + 1 more.
- Prompt size (`test/prompt-baseline.json`, 0025): new items `forkPrompt` (384 Korean / 769 English characters), `leadRule` (168 / 329, on every card turn that may ask), `leadPrompt` (781 / 1,478), `leadReply` (252 / 520), `smallOpinion` (330 / 675), `smallConclude` (251 / 523). Existing items are unchanged.
- Checked with fake CLIs in the tests.
