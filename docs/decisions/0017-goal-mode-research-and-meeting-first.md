## Context

After 0014 to 0016 a confirmed goal goes straight into the team's stage list. Nobody first looks up what the goal depends on (rules, similar cases, competitors, what users complain about), and nothing decides what to make before making it; for a contest entry that is most of the work. Meetings so far continue each participant's session conversation: in a long session one meeting turn cost about 12 dollars (API price, 2026-11-11 run), and the turns stay in the agents' conversations.

## Options

1. Leave research and meetings to the team's own stages. A team may have none, and their answers would reach later stages as earlier results, not marked as reference.
2. Let the lead decide during the run what to research and whether to meet. The order and the number of calls can't be predicted or tested, and 0014 keeps the order in code.
3. Fixed steps in code before the stage list (scan, questions, research per question, a research note, a meeting), each one the user can switch off on the card; every turn runs once in a new conversation; their text reaches the work only as marked reference.

## Decision

Option 3.

- **Steps and order** (fixed in code): with research on, **Scan** (the first research agent, one call: the topic, the current state, sources to start from, assumptions to check) → **Research questions** (the lead writes 2 to 6 questions in one fenced `oc-research` JSON block at the end of the answer; questions are made one line, up to 200 characters, duplicates dropped, at most 6; one question is accepted; a block that can't be read is asked for once more in the same run with a new message, and if that fails too, or the lead ends in an error or is skipped, the run goes on with one question, "Facts we need to know first to reach this goal", and says so) → **Research** (one call per question; questions are handed to the research agents in turn, agents work at the same time, one agent's questions one after another) → a research note. With the meeting on, **Meeting** (the lead as chair, the 0010 and 0011 engine as it is, 2 rounds). Then the team's stages and the criteria check (0015). These are stages at the front of the work run with `mission: 'scan'|'questions'|'research'|'meeting'`.
- **Who**: research agents are every team member with a research role, in team order; meeting participants are the lead and the team members who aren't builders or reviewers, in team order, at most 5. Nobody outside the team (so a team of local models stays local, 0007).
- **The card**: `prep: { direct, research: { can, on, who, min, max }, meeting: { can, on, who, rounds, calls, checks } }`. Both boxes are on by default, and off by default when the lead put `"direct": true` in the goal block (the goal prompt mentions it only to teams that can do either step); the card then says the lead suggested starting right away. A step the team can't do is locked with the reason (no research role; fewer than two participants). The card shows an estimate for the chosen steps. Confirming sends `prep: { research, meeting, researchers, attendees }` (the boxes and the lists the card showed); a step the team can't do now, or a list that differs from the current one, gets 409 with the current `prep`, and nothing starts. A confirm without `prep` (an older screen) runs no research and no meeting. The goal stores `mission.prep`; the confirmed card keeps `prepChosen`.
- **Run once, new conversations**: every call in these steps uses the run-once mode of 0015 (no conversation id read or saved, no usage baseline read or written, no role-note hash, no earlier conversation, memory, handoff note or whole-conversation file, no question card, no queued instructions), and their answers, the pre-work meeting's turns included, are marked `prep` and kept out of project memory (a team's own meeting stage in a goal still goes into memory as before). Every meeting inside a goal's work run, the pre-work meeting and the team's own meeting stages (with their research turns), runs once too: each turn gets the agenda, the research note when there is one, and everything said so far, never the participant's earlier conversation. Meetings outside goal mode are unchanged. Messages that ran once are marked `once`; like review answers they don't count as an agent's own last answer (missed conversation, handoff writer).
- **Prompts**: only the confirmed goal and criteria are instructions (the same block the work stages get), with the user's request and attached files. The scan, the questions the lead wrote, the research answers and the meeting conclusion are reference inside random markers. Research prompts ask for a source on every fact, "not checked" for what wasn't verified, and no file changes. The questions prompt lists, for planning goals, rules and conditions / similar cases / competing services / past winners / user complaints, to use only where they fit.
- **Research note**: `mission.research = { scanMsgId, questions: [{ id: 'q1', text, agentId, status: 'pending'|'running'|'done'|'empty'|'failed'|'skipped', msgId, outside? }], noteMsgId, fallback? }`. A branch that fails, is skipped or answers empty is "not checked" and the others go on. The code then adds one card message `{ role: 'proposal', kind: 'research', status: 'done', text: '', missionId, total, missing, fallback?, questions: [{ id, text, agentId, status, msgId, excerpt, more }] }`: "n of m questions not checked", the first 1,200 characters of each answer and a link to the whole answer, marked "written by an agent · not confirmed by you". It is not passed to agents and not stored in memory.
- **Where it goes**: the research note (each answer's first 1,200 characters) goes to the pre-work meeting and every work stage; the conclusion (`mission.meetingMsgId`, first 2,000 characters) goes to every work stage; both as reference blocks, neither into the criteria check, and the steps are not repeated under "[Previous stage results]".
- **Extra instructions** sent during these steps are stored as before and go into the work stages, not into the research or the pre-work meeting. An instruction sent from the panel to an agent in one of these steps, or in a goal meeting turn, becomes an extra instruction (there is no conversation to put it into).
- **Calls**: every call counts toward the goal's cap. Before each step the run checks what it needs: the scan 1, the questions 1, the research the open questions, the meeting `p + (rounds − 1)(p − 1) + max(0, rounds − 2) + 1` for `p` participants (the meeting engine's count without research turns), with the same cap question as 0015. When the calls left are under a quarter of the cap, research during a goal meeting is turned off (a question block gets "not checked", `check.status: 'low'`, no call) with one log line per meeting.
- **Cut and continue** (0016): `mission.at.phase` can also be `scan`, `questions`, `research` or `meeting`. Continuing starts at that step; earlier steps don't run again; research runs only the questions that didn't finish (`pending`, `running`); a meeting starts again at round 1. A cap question is asked again without a call; any other wait in a step (an error in the meeting) redoes the step. No conversation needs dropping (everything ran once). The stage list check compares the steps' kinds and positions (not their labels).
- **Screen**: the stage strip shows "Scan → Research questions → Research ×n → Meeting → team stages → Criteria check"; the questions answer hides its block and lists the questions; research answers carry their question id; turns that ran once show "new conversation"; the research note card; the progress tab shows the choice, the note summary and a line per step.
- The demo provider answers the scan, the questions and the research by their exact heads and closing block, so the steps can be tried at no usage. The fake Claude for tests reads `RQBAD`, `RQBAD2`, `RFAIL`, `REMPTY`, `DIRECT` and `SLOWSCAN`/`SLOWQ`/`SLOWR2`/`SLOWMEET`.

This adds `mission.prep`, `mission.research`, `mission.meetingMsgId` and four `at.phase` values; `prep`, `once` and `researchBlock` on messages; `prep` and `prepChosen` on goal cards; `kind: 'research'` cards; `count` and four `mission` values on run stages. Older versions don't know them; they draw a research note card as "Hire proposal cancelled".

## Consequences

- A goal with both steps costs 2 + the questions (2 to 6) + the meeting (4 for two participants, 6 for three, plus up to 3 research turns) more calls; the demo team's goal went from 6 to 18 calls.
- A goal's meetings no longer continue the participants' conversations: they don't pay for a long session's history in every turn, and they don't remember the meeting in their own conversation later (the work stages get the conclusion as reference). Continuing a cut team meeting still starts every participant fresh (0016), although the meeting itself didn't use their conversations.
- Not covered: the meeting can't ask the user and there are no two branches (stage 3). No task cards (2c). Whether a read-only research agent can search the web depends on the CLI and isn't checked. Sources in research answers aren't checked by code. Extra instructions sent during the research or the pre-work meeting reach only the work stages. The team's own meeting stages are still checked against the number of participants before they start, not against the meeting's call count.
- Not covered: how real CLIs follow `oc-research` and how good their research is isn't measured; the tests use fake CLIs only.
